Job Title: Construction Project Manager / Construction Engineer

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled and organized Construction professional to plan, coordinate, and oversee construction projects from inception to completion. The ideal candidate will have experience in project management, site supervision, and construction safety.

Key Responsibilities:

Plan, schedule, and manage construction projects, ensuring timely completion.

Oversee construction sites, supervise staff, and coordinate subcontractors.

Ensure compliance with safety regulations, building codes, and quality standards.

Prepare and manage project budgets, procurement, and resources.

Conduct site inspections, monitor progress, and resolve on-site issues.

Collaborate with architects, engineers, and stakeholders on project requirements.

Maintain project documentation, reports, and progress records.

Implement process improvements and risk management strategies.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Management, or related field.

Proven experience in construction project management or site supervision.

Strong knowledge of construction methods, materials, and safety regulations.

Excellent leadership, communication, and organizational skills.

Proficiency in project management and CAD software.

Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in project management (e.g., PMP).

Experience with large-scale commercial or infrastructure projects.

Knowledge of sustainable construction practices and modern building technologies.
