Job Title: Business Development Executive / Manager

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a results-driven Business Development professional to identify new business opportunities, build relationships, and drive revenue growth. The ideal candidate will have strong sales, networking, and strategic planning skills.

Key Responsibilities:

Identify and pursue new business opportunities and potential clients.

Build and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective clients.

Conduct market research to understand trends, competitors, and customer needs.

Prepare and deliver presentations, proposals, and business pitches.

Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to develop strategies.

Negotiate contracts, agreements, and pricing with clients.

Track and report on business development performance metrics.

Participate in networking events, conferences, and industry exhibitions.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Finance, or related field.

Proven experience in business development, sales, or account management.

Strong communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.

Ability to analyze market trends and identify growth opportunities.

Goal-oriented and self-motivated with strong problem-solving skills.

Proficiency in CRM tools and MS Office applications.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in strategic partnerships or B2B sales.

Knowledge of digital marketing and lead generation techniques.

Familiarity with financial analysis and forecasting.
