Job Title: Healthcare Professional / Medical Officer / Healthcare Specialist

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a compassionate and skilled Healthcare Professional to provide medical care, support patient well-being, and ensure compliance with healthcare standards. The ideal candidate will have experience in patient care, clinical procedures, and healthcare management.

Key Responsibilities:

Provide direct patient care and medical consultation.

Diagnose and treat illnesses, injuries, and medical conditions.

Maintain accurate patient records and document treatments.

Collaborate with other healthcare professionals to develop care plans.

Educate patients on health management, prevention, and wellness.

Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations, safety protocols, and hygiene standards.

Assist in medical research, audits, or community health initiatives.

Monitor and manage medical supplies and equipment.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Medicine, Nursing, Healthcare Administration, or related field.

Proven experience in clinical care, patient management, or healthcare services.

Strong knowledge of medical procedures, treatments, and healthcare regulations.

Excellent communication, empathy, and interpersonal skills.

Ability to work under pressure and handle emergency situations.

Preferred Skills:

Certification or specialization in a specific medical field (e.g., Pediatrics, General Medicine, Nursing).

Knowledge of electronic medical records (EMR) and healthcare software.

Experience in hospital administration, community health programs, or research initiatives.
