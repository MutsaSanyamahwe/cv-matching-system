Job Title: Apparel Production / Fashion Coordinator

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are looking for a detail-oriented Apparel Coordinator to oversee garment production, quality control, and supply chain operations. The ideal candidate will have experience in fashion manufacturing, textile knowledge, and production planning.

Key Responsibilities:

Coordinate and monitor apparel production from design to finished product.

Ensure garments meet quality standards and specifications.

Collaborate with designers, suppliers, and manufacturers to meet deadlines.

Manage inventory of fabrics, materials, and finished products.

Review technical specifications, patterns, and samples.

Monitor production schedules and resolve delays or issues.

Ensure compliance with health, safety, and labor regulations.

Prepare reports on production efficiency, costs, and quality.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Fashion Design, Apparel Manufacturing, Textile Engineering, or related field.

Experience in garment production, apparel coordination, or fashion supply chain.

Strong knowledge of fabrics, patterns, and garment construction.

Proficiency in apparel design software (e.g., CAD, Illustrator) is a plus.

Excellent organizational, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Detail-oriented with a focus on quality control.

Preferred Skills:

Knowledge of sustainable fashion practices.

Experience with vendor management and sourcing.

Familiarity with fashion industry trends and standards.
