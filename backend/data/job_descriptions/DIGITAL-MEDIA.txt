Job Title: Digital Media Specialist / Digital Media Executive

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a creative and tech-savvy Digital Media Specialist to manage and develop digital content across various online platforms. The ideal candidate will have experience in digital marketing, content creation, and social media management to enhance brand visibility and engagement.

Key Responsibilities:

Create, manage, and publish digital content for websites, social media, and online campaigns.

Develop and implement digital marketing strategies to increase brand awareness and audience engagement.

Monitor and analyze performance metrics using analytics tools.

Manage social media accounts and interact with online communities.

Collaborate with design, marketing, and content teams to develop campaigns.

Optimize digital content using SEO and digital marketing best practices.

Track trends in digital media and recommend new strategies or tools.

Maintain brand consistency across all digital channels.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Digital Media, Marketing, Communications, or related field.

Experience in digital marketing, social media management, or online content creation.

Knowledge of digital marketing tools, analytics platforms, and content management systems.

Strong creativity, communication, and storytelling skills.

Familiarity with SEO, social media advertising, and content strategy.

Ability to manage multiple digital projects and meet deadlines.

Preferred Skills:

Experience with graphic design or video editing tools (e.g., Adobe Creative Suite).

Knowledge of paid advertising platforms (Google Ads, social media ads).

Understanding of data analytics and digital campaign performance tracking.
