Job Title: Designer / Graphic Designer / Visual Designer

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented Designer to create visual concepts, graphics, and layouts for digital and print media. The ideal candidate will have a strong portfolio, proficiency in design software, and the ability to translate ideas into compelling visuals.

Key Responsibilities:

Develop visual concepts, designs, and layouts for digital and print projects.

Collaborate with marketing, product, and creative teams to understand project requirements.

Create graphics, illustrations, and other visual assets for websites, social media, and promotional materials.

Ensure designs are consistent with brand guidelines and project objectives.

Review and improve existing designs based on feedback and trends.

Prepare files for print or digital publication and manage design assets.

Stay updated on design trends, tools, and industry best practices.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, Visual Arts, Multimedia, or related field.

Proven experience as a Designer, Graphic Designer, or Visual Designer.

Proficiency in design software such as Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign).

Strong creative, conceptual, and artistic skills.

Excellent attention to detail, time management, and communication skills.

Ability to work on multiple projects and meet deadlines.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in UI/UX design, motion graphics, or web design.

Knowledge of HTML, CSS, or other front-end design tools.

Familiarity with branding, marketing, and advertising principles.
