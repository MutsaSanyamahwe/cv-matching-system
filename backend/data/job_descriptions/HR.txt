Job Title: Human Resources (HR) Specialist / HR Officer / HR Manager

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a detail-oriented and people-focused HR professional to manage employee relations, recruitment, and HR operations. The ideal candidate will have experience in HR policies, talent management, and organizational development.

Key Responsibilities:

Manage recruitment, onboarding, and employee lifecycle processes.

Develop and implement HR policies, procedures, and compliance measures.

Handle employee relations, grievances, and conflict resolution.

Conduct performance evaluations, training, and development programs.

Maintain accurate HR records, reports, and documentation.

Support payroll, benefits administration, and compensation planning.

Promote employee engagement, wellness, and organizational culture initiatives.

Ensure compliance with labor laws, regulations, and company policies.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field.

Proven experience in HR roles such as HR Officer, HR Specialist, or HR Manager.

Strong knowledge of HR practices, labor laws, and compliance standards.

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills.

Proficiency in HR software and MS Office applications.

Ability to manage multiple tasks and maintain confidentiality.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in HR (e.g., SHRM, CIPD).

Experience in talent acquisition, employee engagement, or performance management.

Knowledge of HR analytics, workforce planning, and organizational development.
