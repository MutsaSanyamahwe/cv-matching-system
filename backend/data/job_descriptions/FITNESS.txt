Job Title: Fitness Trainer / Personal Trainer / Fitness Coach

Job Type: Full-time / Part-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a motivated and certified Fitness Trainer to design and lead personalized workout programs for clients. The ideal candidate will have strong knowledge of exercise techniques, nutrition, and wellness practices to help clients achieve their fitness goals.

Key Responsibilities:

Assess clients’ fitness levels, goals, and health conditions.

Design and implement personalized workout programs and routines.

Guide clients through exercises, ensuring proper form and safety.

Provide advice on nutrition, wellness, and lifestyle changes.

Monitor client progress and adjust training plans as needed.

Conduct group fitness classes and workshops.

Maintain fitness equipment and ensure a safe training environment.

Stay updated with the latest fitness trends, techniques, and certifications.

Qualifications:

Certification in Personal Training, Fitness Coaching, or related field (e.g., ACE, NASM).

Proven experience as a Fitness Trainer or in a similar role.

Knowledge of anatomy, physiology, and exercise science.

Strong communication, motivational, and interpersonal skills.

Ability to create customized workout and wellness plans.

Physically fit and able to demonstrate exercises.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in specialized training such as strength conditioning, yoga, or Pilates.

Knowledge of nutrition planning and lifestyle coaching.

Ability to manage group classes and motivate diverse clients.
