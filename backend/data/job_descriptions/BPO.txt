Job Title: BPO Customer Service Representative / BPO Executive

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a motivated and customer-focused BPO Executive to handle client interactions, resolve inquiries, and provide high-quality service. The ideal candidate will have strong communication skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced, target-driven environment.

Key Responsibilities:

Handle inbound and outbound calls, emails, or chat interactions with clients.

Provide accurate information and resolve customer inquiries in a timely manner.

Document interactions and maintain detailed records in CRM systems.

Follow company policies, procedures, and service level agreements (SLAs).

Identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction and process efficiency.

Meet individual and team performance targets.

Escalate complex issues to supervisors or specialized teams as needed.

Participate in training sessions and continuous learning programs.

Qualifications:

High school diploma, diploma, or bachelor’s degree in any field.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Basic computer skills and familiarity with CRM software.

Ability to handle high call volumes and multitask efficiently.

Strong problem-solving and interpersonal skills.

Adaptable and able to work in a team-oriented environment.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in BPO, customer support, or call center operations.

Knowledge of multiple languages is a plus.

Familiarity with telecommunication and support tools.

Understanding of quality and performance metrics in a BPO environment.
