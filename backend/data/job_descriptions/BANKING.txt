Job Title: Bank Officer / Banking Specialist

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a detail-oriented and customer-focused Bank Officer to manage financial transactions, provide banking services, and support clients with their banking needs. The ideal candidate will have knowledge of banking operations, compliance, and financial products.

Key Responsibilities:

Process customer transactions, including deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.

Open and manage customer accounts and maintain accurate records.

Advise clients on financial products, services, and investment options.

Ensure compliance with banking regulations, policies, and procedures.

Monitor account activities for unusual or suspicious transactions.

Assist in loan processing, credit evaluation, and risk assessment.

Resolve customer inquiries, complaints, and service issues.

Prepare reports on banking activities, trends, and performance.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Banking, Accounting, or related field.

Experience in banking, finance, or customer service roles.

Strong knowledge of banking products, services, and regulations.

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and problem-solving skills.

Attention to detail and accuracy in financial transactions.

Proficiency in banking software and MS Office applications.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in banking or financial services (e.g., CFA, CPA).

Experience in risk management, loans, or investment advisory.

Knowledge of digital banking platforms and fintech solutions.
