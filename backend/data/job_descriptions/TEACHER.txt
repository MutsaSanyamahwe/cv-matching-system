Job Title: Teacher / Educator / Academic Instructor

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a dedicated and knowledgeable Teacher to educate, guide, and support students in achieving academic success. The ideal candidate will have expertise in their subject area, strong communication skills, and the ability to create an engaging learning environment.

Key Responsibilities:

Develop and deliver lesson plans according to curriculum standards.

Assess, monitor, and evaluate student performance and progress.

Foster a positive, inclusive, and engaging learning environment.

Provide guidance, mentorship, and support to students.

Prepare educational materials, assignments, and exams.

Communicate with parents and guardians regarding student progress.

Participate in professional development and school activities.

Maintain accurate records of attendance, grades, and student behavior.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Education or relevant subject area.

Teaching certification or license where applicable.

Proven experience in teaching or educational instruction.

Strong knowledge of subject matter and curriculum standards.

Excellent communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills.

Ability to manage classroom dynamics and foster student engagement.

Preferred Skills:

Experience with online teaching tools and educational technology.

Ability to develop creative lesson plans and teaching strategies.

Knowledge of student assessment methods and learning techniques.
