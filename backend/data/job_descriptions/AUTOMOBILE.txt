Job Title: Automotive Technician / Automobile Engineer

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled Automotive Technician to inspect, maintain, and repair vehicles. The ideal candidate will have strong technical knowledge of automobile systems and a commitment to safety and quality service.

Key Responsibilities:

Diagnose and repair mechanical and electrical issues in vehicles.

Perform routine maintenance such as oil changes, brake inspections, and tire rotations.

Inspect vehicles to ensure compliance with safety standards and regulations.

Test drive vehicles to identify problems and verify repairs.

Maintain accurate service records and customer reports.

Advise customers on vehicle care, maintenance, and repair options.

Collaborate with other technicians and service staff to improve workflow and efficiency.

Stay updated on latest automotive technologies and repair techniques.

Qualifications:

Diploma or degree in Automotive Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or related field.

Proven experience as an Automotive Technician or Mechanic.

Strong understanding of vehicle systems, engines, and diagnostic tools.

Proficiency in using automotive repair and diagnostic equipment.

Excellent problem-solving and mechanical skills.

Attention to detail and commitment to quality service.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in Automotive Technology (e.g., ASE, NATEF).

Experience with hybrid or electric vehicles.

Knowledge of automobile safety and environmental regulations.
