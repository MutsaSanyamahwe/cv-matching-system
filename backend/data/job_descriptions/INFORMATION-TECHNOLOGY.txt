Job Title: IT Specialist / IT Support Engineer / Systems Administrator

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled and proactive IT professional to manage, maintain, and support our IT infrastructure. The ideal candidate will have experience in network management, systems administration, and technical support to ensure seamless business operations.

Key Responsibilities:

Install, configure, and maintain computer systems, servers, and networks.

Provide technical support and troubleshoot hardware, software, and network issues.

Monitor system performance, security, and backups.

Manage user accounts, permissions, and IT access controls.

Implement IT policies, procedures, and best practices.

Collaborate with other departments to support IT needs and projects.

Maintain documentation for systems, processes, and incidents.

Stay updated on emerging technologies, cybersecurity threats, and IT solutions.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or related field.

Proven experience in IT support, systems administration, or network management.

Strong knowledge of operating systems, networking, and cybersecurity principles.

Proficiency with IT management tools, troubleshooting, and diagnostic software.

Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.

Ability to work independently and handle multiple IT issues simultaneously.

Preferred Skills:

Certifications such as CompTIA, Cisco (CCNA), Microsoft (MCSA/MCSE), or ITIL.

Experience with cloud services (AWS, Azure) or virtualization technologies.

Knowledge of database management, cybersecurity practices, and IT project management.
