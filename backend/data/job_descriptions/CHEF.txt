Job Title: Chef / Head Chef / Culinary Specialist

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled and creative Chef to oversee kitchen operations, prepare high-quality dishes, and ensure excellent culinary standards. The ideal candidate will have experience in menu planning, food preparation, and kitchen management.

Key Responsibilities:

Prepare and cook meals according to recipes, standards, and dietary requirements.

Plan and design menus based on seasonal ingredients and customer preferences.

Manage kitchen staff, assign tasks, and ensure smooth workflow.

Maintain hygiene, safety, and sanitation standards in the kitchen.

Monitor inventory levels, order ingredients, and manage food costs.

Train and mentor junior kitchen staff in culinary techniques.

Ensure presentation and quality of dishes meet the restaurant’s standards.

Innovate new dishes and adapt recipes to improve the menu.

Qualifications:

Diploma or degree in Culinary Arts, Hospitality, or related field.

Proven experience as a Chef, Sous Chef, or Cook in a professional kitchen.

Strong knowledge of cooking techniques, food safety, and kitchen management.

Creativity, leadership, and team management skills.

Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced environment.

Attention to detail and commitment to quality.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in advanced culinary techniques or hospitality management.

Experience in international or specialized cuisine.

Knowledge of menu costing, portion control, and food budgeting.
