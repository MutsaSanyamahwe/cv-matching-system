Job Title: Aviation Specialist / Aircraft Operations Officer

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a detail-oriented and safety-focused Aviation Specialist to manage and support aircraft operations, maintenance, and compliance. The ideal candidate will have knowledge of aviation regulations, aircraft systems, and operational procedures.

Key Responsibilities:

Monitor and manage aircraft operations, including flight scheduling and coordination.

Ensure compliance with aviation safety regulations and standards.

Assist in aircraft maintenance planning and inspections.

Conduct pre-flight and post-flight checks to ensure safety and operational readiness.

Maintain accurate records of flights, maintenance, and operational activities.

Collaborate with pilots, ground staff, and regulatory authorities.

Investigate incidents or operational issues and recommend corrective actions.

Support training programs for aviation staff and crew.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Aviation, Aeronautical Engineering, or related field.

Knowledge of aviation safety regulations and operational procedures.

Experience in aircraft operations, maintenance, or aviation management.

Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.

Proficiency with aviation management software and tools.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in aviation safety or aircraft maintenance (e.g., FAA, EASA).

Experience with commercial or private aircraft operations.

Knowledge of flight planning, air traffic control, and aviation logistics.
