Job Title: Arts Coordinator / Arts Specialist


Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a creative and organized Arts Coordinator to plan, implement, and support arts programs and projects. The ideal candidate will have experience in arts management, creative projects, and community engagement.

Key Responsibilities:

Plan and coordinate arts programs, exhibitions, and events.

Collaborate with artists, educators, and community organizations.

Manage budgets, schedules, and resources for arts projects.

Promote arts initiatives through marketing, social media, and outreach.

Maintain records of events, participants, and program outcomes.

Support artists in project development and grant applications.

Evaluate program effectiveness and recommend improvements.

Ensure compliance with organizational policies and legal requirements.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Fine Arts, Arts Management, Cultural Studies, or related field.

Experience in arts administration, project coordination, or creative programs.

Strong knowledge of arts practices and cultural trends.

Excellent organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills.

Proficiency in office and project management software.

Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in grant writing or fundraising for arts programs.

Knowledge of visual, performing, or digital arts production.

Networking skills within the arts and cultural community.
