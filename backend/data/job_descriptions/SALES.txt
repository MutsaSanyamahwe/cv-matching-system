Job Title: Sales Executive / Sales Representative / Account Manager

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a results-driven Sales professional to generate leads, build client relationships, and drive revenue growth. The ideal candidate will have strong communication, negotiation, and customer service skills.

Key Responsibilities:

Identify and pursue new business opportunities and potential clients.

Build and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective clients.

Present and promote products or services to customers.

Negotiate contracts, pricing, and terms with clients.

Achieve individual and team sales targets.

Prepare sales reports and maintain accurate records in CRM systems.

Collaborate with marketing and product teams to align strategies.

Attend trade shows, exhibitions, and networking events to generate leads.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, or related field.

Proven experience in sales, business development, or account management.

Strong communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.

Goal-oriented with a track record of achieving sales targets.

Proficiency in CRM tools and Microsoft Office applications.

Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in B2B or B2C sales.

Knowledge of sales analytics, forecasting, and lead generation techniques.

Familiarity with digital sales channels and marketing strategies.
