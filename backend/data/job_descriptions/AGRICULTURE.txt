Job Title: Agricultural Specialist / Agriculture Officer

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a knowledgeable and proactive Agricultural Specialist to support farm operations and promote sustainable agricultural practices. The ideal candidate will have experience in crop management, soil health, and modern farming techniques.

Key Responsibilities:

Plan, implement, and oversee crop production activities.

Monitor soil conditions and recommend soil improvement practices.

Advise on the use of fertilizers, pesticides, and irrigation techniques.

Conduct research on new crop varieties and farming methods.

Train farm staff on agricultural best practices.

Maintain accurate records of crop yields, farm activities, and input usage.

Assist in budgeting and cost management for agricultural operations.

Ensure compliance with environmental and safety regulations.

Collaborate with agronomists, suppliers, and other stakeholders.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Agriculture, Agronomy, Horticulture, or related field.

Experience in crop production, farm management, or agricultural consulting.

Strong understanding of soil science, irrigation, and pest management.

Knowledge of sustainable and modern agricultural practices.

Proficiency in farm management software is a plus.

Excellent problem-solving, organizational, and communication skills.

Preferred Skills:

Certification in agricultural management or agronomy.

Experience with mechanized farming equipment.

Knowledge of market trends and crop economics.
