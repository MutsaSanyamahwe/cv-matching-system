Job Title: Public Relations Specialist / PR Officer / Communications Manager

Job Type: Full-time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled and strategic Public Relations professional to manage communication, media relations, and brand reputation. The ideal candidate will have experience in media outreach, content creation, and event management to enhance the organization’s public image.

Key Responsibilities:

Develop and implement PR strategies and campaigns to promote the organization.

Write press releases, speeches, newsletters, and other communication materials.

Build and maintain relationships with media, journalists, and influencers.

Manage social media presence and online reputation.

Organize press conferences, events, and public engagements.

Monitor media coverage and provide reports on PR effectiveness.

Respond to media inquiries and handle crisis communications.

Collaborate with marketing and communication teams on messaging and branding.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Public Relations, Communications, Journalism, or related field.

Proven experience in PR, media relations, or corporate communications.

Excellent written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills.

Strong organizational, project management, and multitasking abilities.

Ability to work under pressure and manage deadlines.

Proficiency in PR tools, social media platforms, and media monitoring software.

Preferred Skills:

Experience in event planning and brand management.

Knowledge of digital marketing, SEO, and social media analytics.

Crisis communication and reputation management experience.
